About This Journal

The Way of Dreams is a personal learning journal documenting the path to becoming a certified Cloud Network Engineer. Every day, notes are added, reviewed, and enriched — turning raw study sessions into a structured, searchable reference.

The curriculum covers networking fundamentals (OSI, TCP/IP, Subnetting) through to cloud-native networking on AWS, Azure, and GCP — including hands-on labs, diagrams, and real exam tips.
